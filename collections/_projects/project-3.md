The MicroMooring is a compact, low-cost, open-source sensor platform designed for monitoring of near-seafloor hydrodynamics and sediment transport. Each unit integrates four trusted sensors into a package weighing under 1 kg, capable of recording wave dynamics, current velocities, and sediment transport over extended periods. Its small footprint and modular design make it scalable for large arrays while maintaining robustness against harsh ocean conditions, including pressures up to 1000 m depth.

The sensor platform incorporates energy-efficient electronics and a tailored microcontroller system, enabling continuous data logging for months without maintenance. Custom circuit boards, waterproof housings, and low-power firmware work together to maximize reliability and minimize cost, making the MicroMooring orders of magnitude more accessible than traditional deep-sea moorings. All components are designed to be easily sourced, assembled, and modified, supporting rapid iteration and deployment by resource-limited research teams.

Beyond its hardware, the MicroMooring is built for openness and reproducibility. The platform’s firmware, schematics, and deployment protocols are fully open-source, allowing other scientists and institutions to replicate or adapt the design for diverse oceanographic measurements. By combining affordability, robustness, and modularity, the MicroMooring provides a new paradigm for seafloor observation, enabling dense, distributed, and high-resolution monitoring that was previously infeasible.
