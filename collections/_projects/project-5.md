When I first arrived at UW, the sediment dynamics group had a project ongoing across the major channels of the Ayeyarwady Delta in Myanmar. I was able to work on this project supporting field collection efforts, and also mapping where land loss and gain had been greatest on the delta over ~50-year timescales. We found the delta front and non-tidal main-stem to be the regions of greatest change, and focused our efforts near the delta front. This mapping project was a nice introduction into how to deploy GIS remote sensing tools at national or global scale.
