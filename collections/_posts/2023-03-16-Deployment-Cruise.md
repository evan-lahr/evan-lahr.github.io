The crew just completed a 12-day cruise offshore the WA margin, aboard the RV Siquliaq. In this time we collected ~20 sediment cores and deployed 6 subsurface instrument platfoms. Needless to say, we are exhausted, but everything went in quite smoothly, and the mountain of data is growing as we speak. Fingers crossed that recovery and turnaround goes smoothly come October.
