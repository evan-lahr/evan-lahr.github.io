If you happen to be a submarine canyon macrobenthos nerd, please help yourself to this footage from our multicorer. One transect per submarine canyon (Astoria, Quinault), at depths ranging from 100-1200 m water depth. Notably, a few of these sites intersect the oxygen minimum zone, where waters exhibit less than 0.15 ml/L dissolved oxygen. Life is persistent!
